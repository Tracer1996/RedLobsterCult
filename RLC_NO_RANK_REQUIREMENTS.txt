RED LOBSTER CULT - NO RANK REQUIREMENTS BUILD
================================================

- The addon opens for every player regardless of guild rank name.
- All members may create raid events and guild events.
- All members may request all guild-bank item categories.
- Shoutout limits are uniform and use the configured daily value.
- Event management remains with the event creator; guild officers may also manage.
- Destructive or guild-wide administration uses native WoW guild permissions, not named ranks.
